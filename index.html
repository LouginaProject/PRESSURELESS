YOUR BLOOD PRESSURE COMPANION

Track your blood pressure with confidence

PRESSURELESS is your blood pressure companion: track your readings, manage your medication, and build healthier habits in one place. It brings prescription reading, medication scheduling, reminders, blood pressure trends, lifestyle journaling and medical reports together in one place.

♥
The goal

Support patients in managing their health, reduce missed medicines and appointments, improve awareness of hypertension and its complications, encourage healthy habits, and make it easier to track progress and communicate with a doctor.

What makes it different

Unlike a general wellness tracker, Pressureless is designed around blood pressure care, including high and low readings and connects AI-assisted prescription scanning with medication adherence, blood pressure monitoring, lifestyle patterns and shareable medical history.

Apple Health

Connect health data through a supported iPhone app or import readings from a blood-pressure monitor.

A normal HTML website cannot directly connect to Apple Health or read HealthKit data. This requires a native iOS app with your permission to access HealthKit. Apple Watch does not directly measure blood pressure; readings must come from a compatible blood-pressure cuff or monitor and then be written to Apple Health by its companion app.

CSV import expects columns named date, systolic, diastolic and optionally pulse. Check every imported reading for accuracy.
